Welcome to our podcast, The Rest is Accountancy!

Our mission is to share and discuss the latest news, in-depth analysis, pressing issues, and reviews of SaaS products related to accounting. With over 30 years of combined experience as public accountants and practitioners in the industry, we bring a wealth of knowledge and insights to the table.

In this introduction, you'll get to know our hosts and learn what to expect from future episodes. We hope you enjoy it and look forward to having you join us for our first episode!
