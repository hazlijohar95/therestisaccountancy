In this podcast, we delve into the question: Can AI replace accountants? While AI's impact on the accounting profession has been gradual compared to other industries, it's increasingly significant. Hazli, Shukri, and Abu share their experiences leveraging AI, discussing its advantages and potential drawbacks. They explore whether AI will boost the profession or pose challenges, particularly in Malaysia, where the readiness to adopt such technology is still developing.
