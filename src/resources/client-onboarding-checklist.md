> Work through it once for every new client, top to bottom. Keep the ticked copy in the client's file. It records what you checked, who checked it and when.
>
> The checks a firm must make on who a client is, who owns it and where its money comes from are set by law and by your professional body, and they change. The second section leaves them blank on purpose. Write in your own firm's procedures, and have them checked against your professional body's current guidance or by an adviser before you rely on this list.

## Onboarding: [Client name]

- Started: [Date]
- Responsible: [Your name]
- Work agreed, in a line: [The services]

### 1. Before you accept

- [ ] Ask what the client needs, in their own words, and write it down
- [ ] Ask who did their accounts and tax before, and why they are moving
- [ ] Ask for the last two years' accounts and returns, if there are any
- [ ] Check you have the skills and the time to do the work well
- [ ] Check for a conflict with any client you already act for
- [ ] Decide whether you want this client. If you don't, say so now, kindly

### 2. The checks your firm must make

- [ ] Who the client is: [Your firm's identity checks for an individual or a business]
- [ ] Who owns and controls it, for a company or partnership: [Your firm's checks on owners and controllers]
- [ ] Where its money comes from: [Your firm's questions on the source of funds]
- [ ] Your view of the risk the client presents: [Low, medium or high, and why]
- [ ] Who made the checks, and when: [Name], [Date]

### 3. The engagement

- [ ] Scope agreed in writing, on your schedule of services
- [ ] Fee agreed, with how and when it is paid: RM [Fee], [Billed monthly, quarterly or yearly]
- [ ] Engagement letter sent: [Your firm's engagement letter, with terms checked by an adviser]
- [ ] Engagement letter signed and returned: [Date]
- [ ] Previous accountant contacted, with the client's permission, for anything you need to take over: [Your professional body's guidance on this, if it has any]

### 4. Access and records

- [ ] How documents reach you, agreed: [Shared folder, portal or email]
- [ ] Bank statements, or read-only access to the accounts
- [ ] Access to the client's accounting software, or a new file set up: [Software]
- [ ] Documents for the first piece of work collected: [List them]
- [ ] Any authority the client gives you to act for them recorded, with when it ends

### 5. The working relationship

- [ ] Every one of the client's filing dates in your calendar, with a reminder a month ahead
- [ ] The client told who to contact and how quickly you reply: [Name, channel and reply time]
- [ ] The dates you need things from the client, agreed: [Monthly or yearly dates]
- [ ] Invoicing set up: [Monthly in advance, quarterly or on completion]
- [ ] First check-in booked: [Date]

### 6. Closing the file

- [ ] This checklist, the checks and the signed engagement letter filed together
- [ ] Anything unusual noted for whoever picks up the client next: [Notes]
