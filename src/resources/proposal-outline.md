> Send it within two working days of the first call, while the call is fresh for both of you. Two pages is enough. Longer proposals are not read.
>
> The first section matters most. If the client reads it and thinks you understood them, the fee becomes a smaller question. Use their words from your call notes.
>
> The terms that make this a contract, such as your terms of business, liability and how either side ends the work, belong in your engagement letter, checked by an adviser. The proposal says what you will do and what it costs.

## A proposal for [Business name]

For [Client's name], from [Your name] of [Your firm's name], [Date].

### Where you are

[Two or three sentences, in the client's own words from the call. For example: you have been doing the books yourself at night, the last return went in late, and you are about to take on your first two staff.]

### What you want

[Two or three sentences on the outcome, not the service. For example: the books off your desk, no more late filings, and a clear picture each month of whether you can afford the new hires.]

### How we would help

| | [Option one] | [Option two] | [Option three] |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What we do | [Services] | [Services] | [Services] |
| What you would notice | [The outcome] | [The outcome] | [The outcome] |
| Fee | RM [Amount] a month | RM [Amount] a month | RM [Amount] a month |

We recommend [Option two], because [One sentence tied to something they told you].

### Your first ninety days

1. In the first week: [What you do, and what they send you]
2. By the end of the first month: [The first thing they receive]
3. By the end of the third month: [What will be true by then]

### What we need from you

- [Documents, records or access]
- [One person on your side, and how quickly they reply]

### Why us

[Two sentences on what you do well for businesses like theirs, and one result for a client you can name with their permission.]

### To go ahead

Reply to this email with the option you choose. We will send the engagement letter and the schedule of services the same day, and start once both are signed.

This proposal is open until [A date two weeks from today].
