> This is for you, not the prospect. Keep it open on the call and write the answers in. Aim for thirty minutes, with the prospect talking for twenty of them.
>
> You are finding out three things: what they need, whether you are the right firm for it, and what it is worth to them. Do not quote on the call. Say when the proposal will arrive, and send it by then.

## First call: [Prospect's name], [Business name]

- Date: [Date]
- How they found you: [Referral, search, a talk, a post]

### Opening

> Thank you for making the time. I'd like to spend most of this call listening, so I understand the business and what you need. At the end I'll tell you how I would help and what happens next. Is half an hour still all right?

### The business

1. Tell me about the business. What do you sell, and to whom?
2. How long has it been trading, and how is it set up? [Sole trader, partnership or company]
3. How many people work in it, including you?
4. Roughly what does it turn over in a year? [Their range]
5. What does a year look like? Are some months much busier than others?

### What they have now

6. Who looks after your books, accounts and tax today?
7. What works about that, and what doesn't?
8. What made you look for someone now?
9. Is everything that should have been filed, filed? If not, what is behind?
10. What do you use for invoicing and the books? [Software, spreadsheets or paper]

### What they want

11. If we were working together a year from now, what would be different?
12. What decision is coming in the next twelve months? [Hiring, borrowing, a new outlet, selling]
13. How often do you want to hear from your accountant, and how?
14. What has an accountant done before that you never want again?

### Fit and budget

15. Who else decides on this, and who signs?
16. Are you speaking to other firms?
17. What have you paid for this kind of work before? [Their answer, without comment]
18. When do you need someone in place?

### Closing

> Thank you. From what you've told me, I'd suggest [The package or work you have in mind]. I'll send a written proposal by [Day], with the fee, what it covers and what I'd need from you. If I don't think we're the right fit, I'll say so and suggest someone who might be.

### After the call

- [ ] Notes written up within the hour
- [ ] Decision made: [Propose, refer elsewhere or decline]
- [ ] Proposal sent by: [Date]
- [ ] Follow-up in the calendar for: [Date]
