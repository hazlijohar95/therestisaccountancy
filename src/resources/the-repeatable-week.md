> Plan the week you want to repeat, not the week you had. The shape below is a starting point that suits most small practices: focused client work in the mornings, calls and email after lunch, and one block a week kept for the firm itself. Change any block that does not fit your clients.
>
> Run it for four weeks before you change it. Each time the plan breaks, note what broke it, and fix the cause rather than the week.

### What each block is for

| Block | What happens in it | What never happens in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client work | Work that needs focus, such as accounts, returns and reviews | Email and calls |
| Calls and email | Replies, client calls and quick questions | Anything that needs an hour |
| Sales | First calls, proposals and follow-ups | Client work |
| Invoicing and admin | Invoices, chasing, filing and payroll | New work |
| The firm | Pricing, systems, writing and the plan | Client work |
| Plan the week | Check what is due and book client work into the week | Client calls |
| Close the week | What got done, what moved and what to change | New work |
| Learning | A course, a session or reading | Email |
