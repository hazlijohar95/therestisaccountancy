> Three packages, not two and not five. Most clients choose the middle one, so build it first: the package you would sell to most of your clients. Then take things out for the first and add them for the third.
>
> Price each package from your floor up, never from a competitor's price down. A package must earn at least your floor times the hours it takes. Check each one against your [client margin worksheet](/members/resources/client-margin-worksheet) before a client sees it.
>
> Name the packages for what the client gets. Gold, silver and bronze tell them nothing.
