> Take ideas from your client work: the question three clients asked this month, the mistake you fixed twice, the decision a client got right. Never use a client's name or figures without their written permission.
>
> Write the long version first. The short versions are cuts from it, not new work.

- Who it is for: [The client your positioning sentence describes]

## The long version, written once

About 600 words, for your letter to clients or an article.

1. The question, as a client would ask it: [Question]
2. The short answer: [Two sentences]
3. Why it matters, with an example: [An example with every detail that identifies a client removed]
4. What to do, step by step: [Three to five steps]
5. When to get help: [The point at which the reader should speak to an accountant]
