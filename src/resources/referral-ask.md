> Ask when a client has just said something good about your work: a deadline met early, a problem solved, a saving found. That is the moment, not the annual review.
>
> Ask for one kind of introduction, not "anyone you know". The more exactly you describe the business you want, the easier it is for the client to think of one.
>
> If you thank clients with a gift or a fee for an introduction, check first what your professional body allows, and be open with your clients about it.

## What to say

> I'm glad that helped. Can I ask you something? Most of my best clients come through introductions from clients like you. Do you know another [Type of business, such as a family-run trading company] whose owner [Has the problem you solve, such as is doing their own books and falling behind]?
>
> If someone comes to mind, would you be happy to introduce us by email? I'll take it from there, and I won't chase them.

If they say yes:

> Thank you. I'll send you a short note you can forward, so it takes you a minute.

If they can't think of anyone:

> No problem at all. If someone mentions it, you know where I am.

## The note they forward

Send this to your client, ready to forward.

**Subject:** An introduction to [Your name], accountant

Hello [Their contact's first name],

I'd like to introduce you to [Your name] of [Your firm's name], who has looked after our accounts and tax for [Number] years. [One specific thing, such as: they had our books up to date within a month.]

You mentioned [The problem their contact has]. I think you two should talk.

[Your name] is on [Your email] and [Your phone number].

[Client's name]

## Your reply when the introduction arrives

**Subject:** Re: An introduction to [Your name], accountant

Thank you, [Client's first name]. I'll move you to bcc.

Hello [Contact's first name],

Good to meet you. [Client's first name] mentioned [The problem]. I'd be glad to hear more. Are you free for twenty minutes on [Day] or [Day]?

[Your name]

## Afterwards

- [ ] Thank the client within a day, whatever comes of it: [A call, a note, or a gift your professional body allows]
- [ ] Tell the client how it went, without sharing anything their contact told you in confidence
- [ ] Record the introduction: [Client], [Contact], [Date], [What came of it]
