> Send the first a week after the proposal, the second a week after that and the third two weeks later. Then stop. A prospect who has not answered three short emails has answered.
>
> Each email asks one question that can be answered in a line. None of them offers a discount, and none of them says "just checking in".

## Email one, a week after the proposal

**Subject:** Your proposal from [Your firm's name]

Hello [First name],

I wanted to make sure the proposal reached you, and to ask whether anything in it needs explaining.

If it would help, I'm free for fifteen minutes on [Day] or [Day] to go through it.

[Your name]

## Email two, a week later

**Subject:** One question on your proposal

Hello [First name],

I know [Business name] keeps you busy, so just one question: is now the right time, or would it be better to pick this up in [Month]?

Either answer is fine. It helps me plan.

[Your name]

## Email three, two weeks later

**Subject:** Closing your file for now

Hello [First name],

I haven't heard back, so I'll take it that now isn't the right time, and close the file on my side.

If anything changes, reply to this email and we'll pick up where we left off. The proposal stands until [Date].

All the best with [Something they told you about, such as the new outlet or the busy season].

[Your name]
