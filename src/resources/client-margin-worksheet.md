> Fill this in straight after the lesson on your floor rate. You need your floor from the [rate tool](/tools/rate) and an honest count of each client's hours, emails, calls and chasing included. If you keep no time records, estimate now, keep a timesheet for one month, then fill it in again.
>
> An example: a client who pays RM 400 a month and takes 4 hours pays RM 100 an hour. At a floor of RM 136, that client is RM 144 a month short.
>
> This sheet is for you. It never goes to a client.

**Fee an hour** is the fee a month divided by the hours a month. For a client on a yearly fee, divide the fee by twelve first.

**Short a month** is your floor times the client's hours, less the fee. Leave it blank when the client pays at or above the floor.
