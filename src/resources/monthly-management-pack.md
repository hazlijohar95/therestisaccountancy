> A pack gets read when its first page answers the owner's questions. Agree those questions with each client at the start. They are usually how the month went, how much cash there is, and what needs a decision.
>
> Send it on the same working day every month. A pack that arrives on time and says less beats a perfect one a fortnight late.

## Management pack: [Client name], [Month and year]

### Before you start

- [ ] Bank and card accounts reconciled to the month-end statements
- [ ] The month's sales and purchase invoices entered, and anything missing listed for the client
- [ ] Payroll for the month posted
- [ ] Accruals and prepayments posted for anything large that falls across months
- [ ] Depreciation posted
- [ ] Loan and hire purchase balances agreed to the lenders' statements
- [ ] Stock taken from the client's count, or estimated: [How you estimated it]
- [ ] Suspense cleared, or each item in it listed with a question for the client

### Page one, the summary

- [ ] Three to five sentences, in plain words, on how the month went
- [ ] Sales, gross margin and profit for the month and the year to date, against the budget and last year
- [ ] Cash at the bank at month end, and the lowest it is likely to be in the next three months
- [ ] What needs a decision, and by when: [Decision and date]

### The reports

- [ ] Profit and loss: the month, the year to date, the budget and last year
- [ ] Balance sheet at month end, beside last month's
- [ ] Cash flow for the month: where the cash came from and where it went
- [ ] Debtors by age, with the largest overdue named
- [ ] Creditors by age, with anything overdue named
- [ ] The three to five measures this client watches: [Measures, such as covers a night, average order value or billable hours]

### The notes

- [ ] Every movement over [An amount or a percentage] explained in a sentence
- [ ] Anything one-off marked as one-off
- [ ] Questions for the client kept apart from the commentary

### Before it goes

- [ ] Profit for the period agrees between the profit and loss and the balance sheet
- [ ] Cash on the balance sheet agrees to the bank statements
- [ ] Every figure on page one agrees to the report behind it
- [ ] Read by someone else, or read aloud by you, for sense
- [ ] Sent by [The working day you agreed], as a PDF and in whatever the client reads it in: [Format]
- [ ] A call booked to go through it: [Date]
