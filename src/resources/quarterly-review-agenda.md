> Send the agenda with the latest pack and forecast three working days ahead, so the client arrives having read them. Keep to the hour.
>
> A review is advice, and advice is charged for. Put it in the client's package or quote for it. A review given away every quarter teaches a client that your thinking is free.

## Quarterly review: [Client name]

- When: [Date], [Time], at [Place or video link]
- Who: [The client's people] and [Your name]
- Papers: management pack to [Month end] and the cash forecast to [Month]

### 1. Since we last met, 5 minutes

- Actions from the last review: [Done, under way or dropped]
- What has changed in the business: [The client to say]

### 2. The quarter in numbers, 15 minutes

- Sales, margin and profit against the budget: [Ahead or behind, and by how much]
- What made the difference: [Two or three reasons]
- The measures we agreed to watch: [Each measure and how it moved]
- Debtors and creditors: [Anything overdue]

### 3. Cash and the next twelve months, 15 minutes

- Cash today: RM [Amount]
- The lowest point in the forecast: RM [Amount], in [Month]
- What the forecast assumes: [Sales, costs and the timing of large payments]
- What would change it: [The risks, and the opportunities]

### 4. Tax this year, 5 minutes

- Where the year's profit is heading: RM [Estimate]
- What to decide before the year ends: [Decisions your planning points to]
- Dates to keep: [The client's own filing and payment dates, from your calendar]

### 5. One decision, 15 minutes

The question the client brought, or the one the numbers raise: [Decision]

- The options: [Two or three]
- What each would do to profit and cash: [The effect of each]
- What we recommend, and why: [Recommendation]
