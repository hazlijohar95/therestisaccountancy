N°2

Logos

These are vector paths from the designer's files. Use them as they are. Never retype either name in a font.

Ground test

Clear space

Keep x clear on every side, where x is the height of the tape.

Minimum size

Podcast lockup 140px or 35mm. Below that, drop the tagline. Club wordmark 110px or 28mm. "The" is the first part to go unreadable.

Which lockup

Tagline when the logo is the hero, lockup everywhere else, wordmark when the tape won’t fit. Reversed on black. Mono inherits the text colour, for single-colour printing and UI.

Never

Don't stretch it, recolour the tape, tilt the lockup, set it on a busy photo or retype it in Fraunces.

N°11

Voice

The brand has written two lines so far: "Death, Taxes and Ai." and "News. Analysis. Interviews." Both are short and certain, end in full stops and carry a dry joke. Write everything else to that standard.

Full stops, not hype.

Short declaratives. One idea a line. No exclamation marks.

yesThree hosts. One mic. The numbers behind the news.noThe ultimate must-listen podcast for finance pros!!

Ask the real question.

A title is the question a practitioner asks over lunch, not a search phrase.

yesWill AI sign the audit report?noAI in Auditing: Key Trends and Insights for 2026

Dry, never snide.

Joke about the work. Never about clients, students or people who trusted you with their books.

yesDeath and taxes were certain. Then AI joined.noClients who can't read a P&L, a thread

Local and exact.

Say RM, LHDN, SST and MPERS when you mean them, and explain each once. Give every figure its unit and period.

yesRM 48,200 in fees for Q3 2026noa big chunk of revenue recently
